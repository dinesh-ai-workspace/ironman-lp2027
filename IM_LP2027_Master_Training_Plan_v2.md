## 1. Scoring System

### Session Importance Tiers

| Importance | Points per Session |
|---|---|
| KEY (K) | 10 |
| Supporting (S) | 4 |
| Optional (O) | 1 |

### Completion Tiers (applied to each session)

| Completion | Points Earned |
|---|---|
| ≥85% of target duration | 100% of session points |
| 70–84% of target duration | 60% of session points |
| 50–69% of target duration | 25% of session points |
| <50% of target duration | 0 points |

**Week Score** = Points Earned ÷ Points Possible × 100

### Grade Scale

| Grade | Score |
|---|---|
| A | ≥90 |
| B | ≥75 |
| C | ≥60 |
| D | ≥45 |
| F | <45 |

### Warnings (no point deduction)

- Any single session exceeds 130% of its target duration
- Any discipline falls below 75% of planned weekly minutes for 2 consecutive weeks

### Cross-Week Matching

KEY sessions only: a ±2-day window may cross week boundaries for logging purposes.
